Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Prism ("we," "us") is a festival-companion app that helps you build a schedule, coordinate with your crew, and find your way around a festival. This page explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressTo send your sign-in link and account-related emails. We don't use passwords.
Display name, handle, avatar colorShown to people you share a crew or conversation with.
Crew data (names, membership, invite codes)To coordinate a group at a festival.
Direct messages and attachmentsTo let you message crew-mates. Stored so the conversation persists across sessions.
Camp pins (map coordinates + an optional note)So your crew can find your campsite or a meetup spot.
Packing checklist stateSo your checklist is there next time you open the app.
Schedule picks (which sets you've added to your own schedule)To build your personal lineup and show your crew who's seeing what.
Spotify data (optional — only if you connect Spotify)Your top genre and top-20 artist names are used to compute a "match %" against festival lineups and crew-mates. We never see your Spotify password. Access tokens are stored server-side and never exposed to other users or to your device.
Push notification subscription (optional)To send you a notification, e.g. for a new DM — only if you turn notifications on.
Blocks and reportsIf you block or report someone, we keep a record so the block is enforced and the report can be reviewed.

Who can see what

  • Direct messages are visible only to the two people in that conversation.
  • Camp pins, Spotify match data, and schedule picks are visible to anyone you share a crew with for that same festival — never to strangers, and never across a festival you don't share.
  • Your profile (name, handle, avatar) is visible to crew-mates and to anyone you message.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Third parties we use

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and file storage provider.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider.
  • Resend — sends transactional emails (sign-in links, confirmations) on our behalf.
  • Spotify — only if you choose to connect your account, governed by Spotify's own privacy policy for the authorization itself.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, crew memberships, messages, camp pins, packing lists, Spotify connection, schedule picks, and every other record tied to your account, immediately and without a recovery option.

One thing worth knowing before you do: if you're the creator of a crew, deleting your account disbands that crew for every member, not just you.

Children's privacy

Prism is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change to how we handle your data, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
